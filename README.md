# 🌍 Hamari Pahchan NGO 

## 📌 Overview

**Hamari Pahchan NGO** is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities through education, digital literacy, healthcare awareness, and social welfare initiatives. The organization focuses on bridging the gap between opportunity and access for marginalized groups, especially children and women.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To create an inclusive society where every individual, regardless of their socio-economic background, has access to education, digital tools, and opportunities for growth.

---

## 👁️ Vision

A future where no child is deprived of education due to lack of resources, and every individual can participate in the digital world with confidence and dignity.

---

## 🚀 Key Initiatives

### 1. Gadgetshala 📱💻

* A digital classroom initiative
* Provides access to laptops, internet, and digital learning tools
* Focuses on children from slum and underserved communities

### 2. Digital Literacy Programs

* Teaches basic computer skills
* Promotes safe and effective internet usage
* Helps bridge the digital divide

### 3. Education Support 📚

* Mentorship programs
* Academic assistance
* Scholarship guidance

### 4. Women Empowerment 👩‍🎓

* Skill development workshops
* Awareness campaigns
* Financial independence initiatives

### 5. Health & Hygiene Awareness 🏥

* Workshops on sanitation and hygiene
* Menstrual health awareness drives
* Community health camps
